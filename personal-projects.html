Personal Projects

Ski Mountain Shelf

Personal BuildFall 2025

My girlfriend saw a ski-mountain-shaped shelf at a friend's house and decided she needed one of her own, so I built it for her. I cut the mountain profile out of wood, wood-burned trail lines and terrain detail into the face, and finished it with a stain to match her space.

It's a good example of how I like to work outside of engineering: start from someone else's idea, then figure out how to actually build the thing.

American Flag Quilt

Dublin Spice Bag Seasoning

R&D ProjectSpring 2025

While visiting my girlfriend in Dublin, Ireland, I had a Chinese-Irish street food dish called a spice bag and couldn't stop thinking about it. The seasoning isn't sold anywhere, so once I got home — 3,000 miles away — I started reverse-engineering it myself, testing spice combinations until I landed on a mix that matched what I'd had in Dublin.

It's become a staple in my kitchen, and everyone who tries it asks for the recipe.
